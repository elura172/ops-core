# Corvus Antiquarian — Bookstagram & Readergram Viral Flywheel Playbook

## 1. How the Viral Product Loop Works

Every time an antiquarian dealer, indie bookshop, or collector clicks **`📸 IG Drop`** in **Corvus Antiquarian (`ops-core`)**:

1. **Instant 4:5 Collector Spec Card (PNG)**: Generates a 1080×1350 Instagram portrait card in three collector palettes (*Obsidian & Gilt*, *Vellum & Ink*, *Oxblood Morocco*) featuring the book's title, author, format/imprint, ABAA condition grade, offering price, and a built-in verification footer:
   `✦ Verified via Corvus Antiquarian · ref:<partner_code>`
2. **Three Ready-to-Post Captions + Niche Hashtag Cloud**:
   - *🕯️ Dark Academia / Literary Collector*
   - *⚡ Rare Vault / First-Edition Spec*
   - *✨ Fresh Stack / Shelf Haul*
3. **1-Click Instagram DM Claim → 48h Priority Courtesy Hold**:
   - When a follower comments `CLAIM` or DMs `@username`, the bookseller enters `@username` inside the Bookstagram Studio and clicks **`🔒 Lock 48h Hold`**.
   - Corvus immediately creates the collector profile, locks a 48-hour courtesy hold, and freezes the SKU (`on_hold`) so AbeBooks, Biblio, or eBay cannot double-sell the volume while the Instagram buyer is completing payment.
